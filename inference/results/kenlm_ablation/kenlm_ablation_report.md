# IISc RESPIN KenLM & Decoding Ablation Benchmark Report

- **Evaluated Test Set**: IISc RESPIN Held-Out Marathi (`meta_test_mr.json`)
- **Total Utterances**: 2,170 (3.04 hours)
- **Target Dialects**: D1 (Malvani/Konkan), D2 (Ahirani/Khandesh), D3 (Standard Marathi), D4 (Varhadi/Vidarbha)
- **Objective**: Determine whether standard 5-gram KenLM aids or degrades dialect speech recognition, and whether lexical prefix-tree decoding or soft LM constraint is superior.

## 1. Overall System Performance Summary (Aggregate ALL)

| Decoder Configuration | CER (%) | WER (%) | SER (%) | Exact Match (%) | RTF | Latency (ms/utt) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Greedy CTC Baseline (alpha=0.0)** | **7.97%** | **32.65%** | 83.82% | 16.18% | 0.0137x | 69.0 ms |
| **Lexicon-Only Beam Search (No LM)** | **7.81%** | **31.85%** | 83.55% | 16.45% | 0.017x | 85.8 ms |
| **KenLM 5-gram (alpha=0.5, default)** | **7.23%** | **28.29%** | 78.11% | 21.89% | 0.0161x | 81.0 ms |
| **KenLM 5-gram Soft (alpha=0.15)** | **6.78%** | **28.06%** | 77.28% | 22.72% | 0.0164x | 82.9 ms |
